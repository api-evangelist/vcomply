Municipal utility compliance is becoming more demanding, more visible, and more difficult to manage with manual processes. Public utilities are no longer responsible only for delivering reliable electricity, water, wastewater, gas, or related services. They also have to prove that every obligation, inspection, policy, safety requirement, permit, report, risk review, vendor activity, and corrective action is being handled on time.
