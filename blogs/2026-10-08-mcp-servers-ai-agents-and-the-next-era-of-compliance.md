Most business software still asks people to work the same way it did years ago: open an application, find the right module, search for what you need, make an update, and move to the next system. For teams working across CRM, finance, HR, security, and compliance platforms, a surprising amount of time disappears into simply moving between tools. AI agents are beginning to change that.
