Public sector organizations must demonstrate how they meet regulatory obligations, use public resources, protect information, and respond to concerns. Their responsibilities span procurement rules, privacy requirements, employment laws, funding conditions, and sector-specific regulations across departments, facilities, contractors, and governing bodies. Changes to these requirements can trigger policy updates and operational adjustments, with an approval in one office affecting how dozens of teams perform their work.
